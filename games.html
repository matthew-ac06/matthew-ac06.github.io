Accident Castle
Submitted for the GADIG GMU Fall Jamfest 2026
My goal for this jam was to improve my game development skills, and my friend wanted to get better at writing music, so we swapped our usual roles and gave each other advice. The prompt for this Jam was "Murphy's Law: Anything that can go wrong, will go wrong," so I immediately thought of developing a rage game with troll mechanics. To set our game apart from normal troll games that rely on memorizing the level, we wanted players to instead have to learn individual mechanics, and tackle them in different situations. To do this, we used a procedural level generator that chooses from a list of level segments, resulting in a "Troll Roguelike."

King Fisher
Developed by GADIG team at GMU for the Spring 2026 semester
I contributed the gameplay theme "Crazy Fish" as well as the associated final minute theme "Psycho Fish." I learned a lot about trying to match a unique genre of music, while combining it with other styles to make it my own. For the Psycho Fish remix, I needed some help to learn about chopping drums, and writing music that focuses more heavily on automation and other production techniques. It was a step out of my comfort zone at the time, but I had a lot of fun and continued to experiment with production-heavy music.
